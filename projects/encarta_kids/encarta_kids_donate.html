Why I'm asking for help

Hello, I'm the person behind Neo Encarta Kids. It began as a side project more than two years ago, and I wanted children to have a safe, simple place to look things up and learn about animals, science, people and places around the world.

Since then, more than 11,000 people have used it. I have built and kept it going largely on my own. The next stage is bigger than one person can carry, and I'd like to build it with support from parents, teachers and anyone who believes learning should be open to every child.

Thank you for reading.
The creator of Neo Encarta Kids, Versecity Tech
