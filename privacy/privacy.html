Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Who we are

VerseCity Technology ("VerseCity", "we", "us") is the parent company of Verse Hub, Verse Lab, Verse Studio and Gold & Gem Verse. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information across our websites and mobile apps.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information — such as name, email address and phone number, where an app requires an account.
  • Usage and device information — such as app interactions, device type, operating system and crash logs.
  • Transaction information — such as airtime purchases, reward redemptions and escrow deals made through our apps.
  • Location data — only in apps that need it, and only with your permission.
  • Content you submit — such as notes, messages and support requests.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and improve our apps, process transactions and rewards, prevent fraud, provide support, send service messages, and meet legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our apps (such as hosting, analytics and payment processors), with participating brands where needed to complete a promo or reward you redeem, with the other party in a Secure Deals transaction where needed to complete it, or where required by law.

5. Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, and we use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children

Our apps are not intended for children without the involvement of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
